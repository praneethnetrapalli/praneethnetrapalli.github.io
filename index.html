Praneeth Netrapalli

 

I am a Research Scientist at Google DeepMind. I am also an adjunct professor at C-MInDS, Mumbai and a faculty associate of ICTS, Bengaluru. Prior to this, I was a researcher at Microsoft Research India for 4.5 years and did my postdoc at Microsoft Research New England in Cambridge, MA. I obtained my MS and PhD in ECE from UT Austin where I was fortunate to be advised by Sujay Sanghavi. Before coming to UT, I worked in the Quantitative Analysis group at Goldman Sachs, Bangalore. I obtained my B-Tech in Electrical Engineering from IIT Bombay.

Honors and awards:

  • INSA Medal for Young Scientists, 2021

  • IEEE Signal Processing Society Best Paper Award, 2019

  • Associate of Indian Academy of Sciences (IASc), 2019–2022

Research

I work on Gemini at Google DeepMind. At GDM, my work has spanned pre-training, post-training and serving, with current focus specifically on post-training for long horizon tasks. In the past, I have worked on designing reliable and robust machine learning (ML) algorithms, representation learning, nonconvex optimization, stochastic optimization, quantum optimization, and minimax/game-theoretic optimization.
